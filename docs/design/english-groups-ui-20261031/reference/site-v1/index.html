A Garden for Everyone

How a small idea brought a neighbourhood together.

1When Maya moved to a new neighbourhood, she noticed an empty piece of land behind the library. People often walked past it without stopping. Maya thought it could become a place where neighbours could grow vegetables and spend time together.

2At first, only five people joined her. They cleared the rubbish, prepared the soil and planted tomatoes. Mr Chen, a retired gardener, showed the children how to care for the plants. Each Saturday, the group met to water the garden and share what they had learned.

3Not everything went well. During a dry week, some plants began to die. Instead of giving up, the group collected rainwater in large containers and made a watering schedule. Soon, more neighbours offered to help.

4By the end of summer, the garden was full of vegetables. The group gave part of the harvest to a local food bank. But Maya felt that the friendships were even more valuable than the vegetables. “We grew something more than food,” she said.
